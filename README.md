# Pitch with Pravin · Formula 1

Formula 1 results, standings and head-to-head, 2021 to the current season.

**Live:** https://analytics99.github.io/pitch-with-pravin-f1/

- **Championship:** the points race round by round, driver standings and constructors.
- **Races:** qualifying, sprint and race classification for every round, with pole, fastest lap and weather.
- **Head to head:** compare any two drivers over a season.
- Works on phones, with a Compact and a Detailed table view.

Follow along:

- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/pitchwithpravin/
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@pitchwithpravin
